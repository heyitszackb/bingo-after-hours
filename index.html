HOW TO PLAY

Each draw pairs three numbered balls with three random empty tiles. Drag a ball onto the card to stamp its highlighted destination. Drag along the track to reorder. Tap balls or spaces to inspect them.

Complete a row, column, full diagonal, the four outer corners, or a filled 2×2 square anywhere. Each space earns 1 point before paint bonuses. All completed patterns score together, then their stamps clear. Tap the grid button or your score to see the patterns and this run’s counts.

Start each stage with 12 calls. Reach its target before calls or balls run out. Played balls leave the bag until the next stage.

Start with $5. Rerolls cost $1. Each unused call pays $1 when you clear a stage.

Between stages, drag $3 paint onto a shop ball. Gold pays $1 when scored. Blue grants 3 extra calls when scored. Red doubles every completed scoring pattern; red paints stack. Paint lasts for the run.
