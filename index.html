HOW TO PLAY

Each draw offers three numbered balls and three random empty spaces. Drag any ball toward any highlighted space; release over the card to stamp the nearest one. Its number and paint stay on the stamp. Drag along the track to reorder. Tap balls or spaces to inspect them.

Bingo scores each tile in a completed row, column, or full diagonal for 1 base point (5 per line). Purchased cards can add bonuses to each scored tile. Drag Bingo into the trash to remove it for the run; rows, columns, and diagonals will no longer score. Each line scores once per stage. Newly completed lines score together; stamps stay on the board until the next stage. Tap the grid button or your score to see the patterns and this run’s counts.

Start each stage with 12 calls. Reach its target before calls or balls run out. Played balls leave the bag until the next stage.

Start with $5. Rerolls cost $1. Each unused call pays $1 when you clear a stage.

Between stages, buy cards or ball upgrades for $3 each. Keep up to five purchased cards alongside Bingo; copies stack. Drag a card to the trash to remove it. Single Digits adds +1 for values 1–9. Outer Layer adds +5 for an edge space. Ball upgrades replace the chosen ball’s previous upgrade. X has no number and can go anywhere empty. Tornado scatters every placed stamp, then checks lines. Dynamite returns all eight neighboring stamps to the bag, keeping itself on the board. Returned balls keep their upgrades and current values. Doubler doubles all eight neighboring numbers before scoring. Values reset each stage; upgrades and cards stay. Refresh all shop offers for $2.
