HOW TO PLAY

One numbered ball rolls out with one random empty space highlighted. Tap Play or drag the ball onto the board to stamp that space, or tap Pass to return the ball to the bag and draw a new ball and location. Passing spends no play or money. Its number and upgrade stay on the stamp. Tap balls or spaces to inspect them.

Bingo scores each tile in a completed row, column, or full diagonal for 1 base point (5 per line). Purchased cards can add bonuses to each scored tile. Drag Bingo into the trash to remove it for the run; rows, columns, and diagonals will no longer score. Each line scores once per stage. Newly completed lines score together; stamps stay on the board until the next stage. Tap the grid button or your score to see the patterns and this run’s counts.

Start each round with 15 plays and 10 passes. Both refill at the start of every round. Reach its target before plays or balls run out. Each placement uses one play. Running out of passes does not end the round; you can still play. Played balls leave the bag until the next stage.

Start with $5. Passes are free to spend. Each unused play pays $1 when you clear a stage.

Between stages, buy cards and ball upgrades. Most cost $3; Number Cruncher costs $7. Each shop offers two different cards. Keep up to five purchased cards alongside Bingo; copies stack. Drag a card to the trash to remove it. Single Digits adds +1 for values 1–9. Outer Layer adds +5 for an edge space. Number Cruncher adds each scored number to points (X adds nothing). Second Wind rolls a five-number range when offered; each scored value in that range restores one play, up to 15. Ball upgrades replace the chosen ball’s previous upgrade. X has no number and can go anywhere empty. Tornado scatters every placed stamp, then checks lines. Dynamite returns all eight neighboring stamps to the bag, keeping itself on the board. Returned balls keep their upgrades and current values. Plasma lets you see the next highlighted space and choose any ball still in the bag. Doubler doubles all eight neighboring numbers before scoring. Values reset each stage; upgrades and cards stay. Refresh all shop offers for $2.
