HOW TO PLAY

One numbered ball rolls out with one random empty space highlighted. Swipe the ball up to stamp the highlighted space. Swipe the ball down to return it to the bag and draw again. Tap the bag below the ball to inspect it without spending anything. You can still drag a ball onto the board. Passing spends no play or money. Its number stays on the stamp. Tap balls or spaces to inspect them.

Your starting cards define the rules. Bingo activates rows, columns, and diagonals of five. Face Value awards each scored tile its ball’s current number in points. Bingo lights up with the full line, then each tile and its contributing cards activate in order. Remove Bingo and lines no longer activate; other trigger cards still work. Remove Face Value and tiles still activate, but no points are awarded. Each line scores once per stage. Stamps stay until the next stage.

Start each round with 17 plays and 10 passes. Both refill at the start of every round. Reach its target before plays or balls run out. Each placement uses one play, except Rocks, which cost no play. Running out of passes does not end the round; you can still play. Played balls leave the bag until the next stage.

Start with $5. Passes are free to spend. Each unused play pays $1 when you clear a stage.

Shop items are free while testing. High Five is a free card: playing a 1–5 scores itself and its occupied orthogonal neighbors. Trigger cards resolve left to right; tiles shared by a line and a cross score for each. Face Value supplies the points. Browse all available purchases by swiping the shop shelf sideways. The 100 Ball permanently lowers the values of occupied orthogonal neighbors by 1 before scoring. Changes stay with pieces for the run, including penalties to future die rolls. Zero and negative values are allowed. Rocks occupy spaces and help complete patterns, but have no number and score zero points. They leave the bag when placed, just like balls. Passing a Rock still uses a pass. Add a Bomb from Items to put a new piece in your bag. Each Bomb draws at the same odds as each numbered ball. On placement, it destroys itself and the items in the eight neighboring spaces for the rest of the run. All ball effects finish before scoring checks the surviving board, so a line broken by the explosion does not score. Empty board spaces remain usable. Passing returns the Bomb to the bag. The 20-Sided Die shows ? until played, then rolls a number from 1 to 20 before scoring. It keeps that value on its space for the round and returns unrolled next round. Choose Next Round to continue.
